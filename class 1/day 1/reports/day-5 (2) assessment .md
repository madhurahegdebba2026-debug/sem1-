# DAY 5 2. YOUR OWN TASK, WALKED AND IMPROVED

## Easy

### Why should a student turn the seven task-path answers into one clear message?
**Answer:** Combining the details into a single structured prompt gives the AI complete context upfront. This prevents misunderstandings, eliminates the need for multiple follow-up turns, and ensures the response is accurate, complete, and tailored to the exact requirements on the first try.

### What does it mean to find one weak step in a first attempt?
**Answer:** It means identifying the specific gap or missing context in your original prompt (such as missing constraints, unclear goals, or lack of background details) that caused the AI's output to be inaccurate, incomplete, or off-target.

### Why is “it looks fine” not enough before trusting an AI answer?
**Answer:** AI models can sound convincing and well-written even when presenting false, outdated, or incomplete information ("hallucinations"). Critical details, calculations, or logic must be manually verified against reliable source materials to ensure correctness.

## Intermediate

### A student asks, "Help me with my assignment." The result is too general. Which task-path step is weak? What should the student add?
* **Weak Step:** Step 1 & 2 (Defining the specific Task/Goal and providing Context/Details).
* **What to add:** The student should add the subject topic, target word count/format, specific assignment instructions, academic level, and what exact output or section they need help creating.

### A student gets a revision timetable but never checks whether the daily workload fits their real pace. What human step did they skip?
**Answer:** They skipped the Verification and Feasibility Check (Review/Evaluate step). They failed to evaluate the output against their personal constraints, real-world availability, and study capacity.

## Hard

### Choose one real task you need this week. Write a clear message for an AI tool using purpose, useful facts, task, and expected result.

#### Example Prompt:
* **Purpose:** Prepare for an upcoming mid-term exam efficiently.
* **Useful Facts:** Course: Financial Accounting (BBA Year 1). I have 3 days left, can study 2 hours per day, and struggle most with Balance Sheet reconciliations.
* **Task:** Create a structured 3-day study schedule focusing on key accounting principles and practice exercises.
* **Expected Result:** A daily breakdown with specific hourly time blocks, target topics, and 3 practice quiz questions per day.

### Your first result is off-target. Name one weak step you would fix, what you would change, and what you would check in the new result.
* **Weak Step to Fix:** Constraints & Context step.
* **What to Change:** Add explicit limitations (e.g., maximum length, target audience level, specific source material, or step-by-step format restrictions).
* **What to Check in New Result:** Verify if the AI adheres strictly to the newly provided constraints, correctly applies the technical details, and delivers the output in the requested format without extra filler.
