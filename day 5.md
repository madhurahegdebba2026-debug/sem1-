# Lesson: Guided AI Task from Need to Checked

## Easy

* **In simple words, what is the task path?**
  * The task path is the step-by-step process of guiding an AI tool from identifying a human need to receiving an AI response, verifying it, and making the final decision.

* **Why should a student decide the purpose and user before asking an AI tool for help?**
  * Deciding the purpose and audience ensures the prompt is specific, helping the AI generate a relevant, accurately tailored response with the right tone and detail.

* **Name the two parts of the task path that always stay with the person.**
  * The human decision (owning the final choice) and the human verification check (verifying the AI's output) always stay with the person.

---

## Intermediate

* **The Naruto club has the hall only from 4 pm to 8:30 pm. Why should this detail be in the AI request?**
  * This constraint prevents the AI from scheduling activities outside the permitted hall availability window, ensuring the timeline is realistic and actionable.

* **The assistant gives an event plan ending at 8:25 pm. What should the club still check before sharing the plan?**
  * The club must verify buffer times for setup and cleanup, check if speakers or equipment need time to clear, and confirm all activity durations add up correctly before the 8:30 pm deadline.

## Hard

* **A student wants help making a revision timetable. Write the seven task-path answers in short points: purpose, user, useful input, AI task, expected result, human decision, and one check.**
  * **Purpose:** Create an effective exam preparation schedule.
  * **User:** A college student preparing for upcoming final exams.
  * **Useful Input:** Exam dates, subjects, topic list, available study hours, and daily break preferences.
  * **AI Task:** Generate a structured, balanced daily revision timetable.
  * **Expected Result:** A clear day-by-day study calendar with subjects allocated based on priority.
  * **Human Decision:** Decide whether to adopt, modify, or reject the timetable based on personal study habits and energy levels.
  * **One Check:** Verify that no study sessions overlap with personal commitments or class hours.

* **Two friends are arguing about who should apologise first. Does the task path fit here? Explain why or why not.**
  * **No, the task path does not fit here.**
  * **Reason:** Personal relationship disputes involve emotions, empathy, shared history, and personal moral values that an AI cannot evaluate or take responsibility for. Interpersonal decisions must be handled directly by the humans involved.
